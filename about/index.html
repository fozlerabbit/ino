About Our Mission

Our Story

Founded in 2024 by visionary young innovator Md. Zahid Hasan Zihad, Chuadanga Science & Robotics Club emerged from a simple yet powerful belief: every student deserves access to quality robotics and STEM education, regardless of their location or economic background.

Starting with no resources, mentors, or financial backing, Zihad transformed a small initiative into a movement that now empowers hundreds of students across multiple districts in Bangladesh. What began as in-person school and college workshops has expanded into a thriving online community through Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and our official website.

Over the years, the club has held more than 100 workshops, seminars, and events, directly benefiting over 5,000 students. Today, we proudly represent Bangladesh in international competitions and continue to promote STEM education, robotics innovation, and social impact.

Md. Zahid Hasan Zihad

Founder & Chief Innovator

Zahid Hasan Zihad is passionate about robotics, innovation, and STEM education. As an HSC student at Darsana Government College, he has dedicated himself to inspiring young minds in Chuadanga through hands-on science and technology initiatives. His vision is to establish a nationwide platform where students can learn for free, share knowledge, and grow together - aiming to benefit over 100,000 students by 2030.

His groundbreaking work includes the Hexaguard Rover, which won Gold at WICE 2025 Malaysia, marking the first international award in Chuadanga District's history. Beyond robotics, Zihad authored "Basic Robotics with AI (Without Coding)" to make technology accessible to beginners, removing coding barriers that often discourage young learners.

Our Mission

To empower 100,000 students across Bangladesh by 2030 through hands-on STEM and robotics education, mentorship, and community engagement - helping them become innovators, problem-solvers, and ethical leaders who create real social impact.

Our Vision

A future where every student in Chuadanga and beyond can access free, high-quality robotics and innovation education, enabling them to solve real-world problems and compete on the international stage.

Core Activities

Our club engages in diverse activities that combine education, innovation, and community service:

  • Robotics Training: Hands-on workshops teaching electronics, programming, and mechanical design
  • Innovation Projects: Developing real-world solutions to community challenges
  • Competition Preparation: Training teams for national and international robotics competitions
  • Community Development: Social initiatives including tree plantation, disaster relief, and educational outreach
  • Mentorship Programs: Connecting students with experienced engineers and educators
