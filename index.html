Empowering Rural Youth through Robotics, Technology & Innovation

Founded in 2024, Chuadanga Science & Robotics Club is on a mission to empower 100,000 students across Bangladesh by 2030 through hands-on STEM and robotics education, mentorship, and community engagement. We're helping young minds become innovators, problem-solvers, and ethical leaders who create real social impact in their communities and beyond.
