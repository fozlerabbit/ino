Hexaguard Rover

Military-Grade Multi-Functional Robotic Rover

A sophisticated robotic rover designed to perform dangerous tasks remotely, reducing human risk in hazardous environments. This innovation showcases advanced engineering and practical problem-solving capabilities.

Key Features:

  • 6-DOF all-metal robotic arm with precision control
  • High-quality live video transmission system
  • Multi-sensor data monitoring and analysis
  • Signal jamming and interference protection
  • Water-pumping and fire-extinguishing modules
  • Injectable sedative/immobilizing system for wildlife
  • Dual-camera setup with night-vision capability
  • Two-way audio communication system
  • Remote control via FlySky FS-i6X and custom app
  • ESP32-based web dashboard for real-time monitoring

Applications:

Bomb detection and defusal, firefighting, military reconnaissance, wildlife sedation, disaster response, gas leak monitoring, and smart security surveillance.

Achievements: Gold Medal at WICE 2025 International (Malaysia), Silver Medal & "Dreams of Bangladesh" Award at WICE 2025 National, Featured at 2nd Ismail Al-Jazari Science Fest 2025

Source: Club Overall Report - Major Projects Section

Medical Booth

Smart Healthcare Access for Rural Communities

An innovative medical information and service booth designed to bridge the healthcare gap in rural and underserved areas, making basic medical services accessible to everyone.

Key Features:

  • Sensor-based health data monitoring
  • Digital report display and record keeping
  • Telemedicine connection capabilities
  • Automated first-aid guidance system
  • IoT-enabled remote doctor consultation
  • Vital signs measurement (temperature, pulse, blood pressure)

Impact:

Enables people in remote areas to check vital health data and connect with medical professionals without immediate doctor presence, revolutionizing rural healthcare accessibility. Particularly valuable in emergency situations and for routine health monitoring.

Source: Club Overall Report - Major Projects Section

AI Booth

AI-Powered Digital Information Hub

A revolutionary digital information hub powered by artificial intelligence, designed to help people access knowledge, government services, and educational resources in their own language.

Key Features:

  • Voice interaction in Bangla language
  • AI-based question answering system
  • Integration with online public databases
  • Educational resource accessibility
  • Government service information portal
  • Digital literacy training modules

Impact:

Empowers students and rural communities with digital literacy, closing the information gap between urban and rural areas through accessible technology. Helps citizens navigate government services and access educational content in their native language.

Source: Club Overall Report - Major Projects Section
