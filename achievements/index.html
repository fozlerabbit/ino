Our Achievements

From local competitions to international recognition, Chuadanga Science & Robotics Club has consistently demonstrated excellence in innovation, bringing pride to Bangladesh on the global stage.

Achievement Timeline

WICE 2025 - International Round (Malaysia)

October 2025

Achieved Gold Medal representing Bangladesh at the World Invention Competition and Exhibition in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. This marked the first international award in Chuadanga District's history, showcasing the Hexaguard Rover to nearly 200 teams worldwide.

WICE 2025 - Bangladesh Round

September 2025

Secured Silver Medal and the prestigious "Dreams of Bangladesh" Special Award at the national round, qualifying for the international competition in Malaysia.

Innovation World Cup 2026 - National Round

2026

Won Gold Medal at the national round and selected for International Round in Indonesia, demonstrating continued excellence in innovation and robotics.

2nd Ismail Al-Jazari Science Fest 2025

2025

Successfully exhibited projects at the National Round, engaging with peers and mentors from across Bangladesh.

National Science & Technology Fair

2024 & 2025

Represented Chuadanga District at both the 45th and 46th National Science & Technology Fairs after qualifying through Upazila, District, and Divisional rounds with 1st Place finishes at each level.

3rd Green Fest 2024

2024

Showcased innovative projects at the National Level, promoting sustainable technology and environmental awareness.
